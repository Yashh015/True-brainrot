Privacy

What the app stores

  • Your feed logins (X, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, Telegram), as an encrypted session on your own PC.
  • Your email, for your True Brainrot account.
  • Three local events from the Claude Code plugin: Claude started working, finished, and needs you.

What it never does

  • It never sees or stores your passwords.
  • It never records your keystrokes.
  • It never records or captures your screen.
